Practice case study · Illustrative

Search Result Relevance Review

A self-directed practice exercise demonstrating a structured way to think about search intent, relevance, source credibility and local context. This is a learning example, not paid search-rating work or an RWS assignment.

  • Example query: “How do I renew a Nigerian international passport?”
  • Review dimensions: relevance, authority, freshness and usefulness.
  • Decision principle: favour current, directly relevant information from an appropriate official source.
This is a demonstration of an evaluation approach. No live search results have been rated here, and it does not represent RWS guidelines or client work.
